# QYM: audited elementary cores, corrected surrogates, and no-go results

This file preserves every public declaration from the original `QYM.lean` compatibility artifact
and integrates independently audited algebraic, finite-dimensional, variational, and no-go source
fragments.  Its scope is deliberately narrower
than the paper *Modular q-Yang--Mills on Admissible Gauge Slices, Modular Flow, and a Spectral
Mass--Gap Mechanism*.

## Status guide

* Declarations directly in `QYM` are **legacy surrogates** retained for compatibility.  They prove
  elementary ring or real-order statements under their displayed hypotheses; they are not faithful
  constructions of a differential graded gauge theory, an unbounded Hamiltonian, or its spectrum.
* Namespaces containing `NoGo` give unconditional elementary obstructions or counterexamples to
  specific proposed inferences.
* `QYM.AffineGauge`, `QYM.ConstantTwoDimensionalDGA`, `QYM.WeightedTraceCore`,
  `QYM.SpectralFlowCore`, `QYM.FiniteHamiltonian`, `QYM.FiniteGalerkinDissipation`,
  `QYM.FiniteDualRieszFlow`, `QYM.AbstractFormSign`, and `QYM.FullCertification.Corrected` contain
  **corrected concrete or algebraic cores**.  They isolate conclusions that follow from their
  displayed finite, algebraic, variational, form-theoretic, or spectral data.
* `QYM.InfiniteDiagonalHamiltonian` is a raw-sequence diagonal sign model.  It is an algebraic
  obstruction for the displayed surrogate, not an analytic realization of the paper's operator.
* `QYM.FinsuppDiagonalQuadraticForm` strengthens that sign audit on the genuine finite-support
  space `ℕ →₀ ℝ`: it proves a quadratic-form lower-bound obstruction for `-n + vₙ` and the
  corresponding lower bound for the corrected `n + vₙ` sign.
* `QYM.FinsuppStrongNormNoGo` separates lower boundedness from strong-norm coercivity, while
  `QYM.SpectralFlowCore.GroundMultiplicityCounterexample` records the failure of an adjacent-level
  gap when the ground eigenspace is degenerate.
* `QYM.SpectralWindowStability` proves finite indexed-spectrum window inclusions and count bounds
  from a pointwise eigenvalue-error estimate; it does not supply the spectra or that estimate.
  `QYM.FiniteSpectralFamilyStability` adds finite-family error, gap, and window consequences under
  similarly explicit hypotheses.
* `QYM.FormSmallPerturbationCore` formalizes the pointwise relative-form inequality, its
  existential B2 coefficient package, and the explicit transferred lower bound.  It does not
  claim KLMN closedness or construct a self-adjoint operator.
* `QYM.RelativeSpectralB2NoGo` proves the energy-dependent B2 error estimate and gives an explicit
  unbounded-spectrum countermodel to an index-independent additive error.  The two-sided relative
  interval itself is retained.
* `QYM.AsymptoticGapLiminf` isolates the complete-order liminf implication behind Corollary 4.25;
  the pointwise gap comparison is an explicit input because Proposition 4.23 does not supply it
  in the presence of ground-state multiplicity.
* `QYM.QuadraticFirstVariationCore` proves the exact affine-line expansion, difference quotient,

  Sobolev differentiability arguments.
* `QYM.TestKernelTailCore` uses `ℝ≥0∞` so infinite smoothed counts and tails are defined without a
  hidden summability hypothesis.  Its window and geometric-tail conclusions expose every
  majorization, counting, decomposition, and decay supplier.

  compact upper support but which admits no finite parameter-uniform inverse-linear natural-tail

* `QYM.DiagonalSpectralDirectionNoGo` proves that the printed `-Δ + V` diagonal sign runs toward
